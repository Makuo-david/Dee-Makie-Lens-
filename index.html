Cinematography is more than operating a camera. It is the deliberate combination of light, composition, movement, lenses, color and timing to shape what an audience feels and notices.

Composition

Where subjects sit inside the frame can create intimacy, tension, scale or isolation.

Movement

Static shots, pans, tilts, tracking and handheld movement each communicate differently.

Light

Light controls atmosphere, depth and attention while helping define the visual language.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a cinematographer do?

A cinematographer shapes the visual approach of a production, working with the director and crew on camera, lighting, composition and movement.
